Our story · เรื่องราวของเรา

Nordic comfort, made with care

Norden brings the flavours of Scandinavia to Chiang Mai, alongside international favourites. Our kitchen is known for Swedish meatballs, gravlax and a lamb shank that falls off the bone.

Find us on the Chiang Mai–Lampang Superhighway in Tha Sala, just before Big C Don Chan: easy to find, plenty of parking, and a warm, welcoming atmosphere for families, friends and couples.
